🌱 Vernal Equinox

📅 March 20

📐 Axial tilt: 23.5° (fixed toward Polaris)

☀️ Subsolar point: Equator (0°)

🌞 Insolation: Equal in both hemispheres

⏳ Daylight: 12 hours (equal day & night)

📐 Scientific Explanation

Earth's axis is tilted 23.5° relative to its orbital plane. This tilt remains fixed (pointing toward Polaris) as Earth revolves around the Sun. When the Northern Hemisphere tilts toward the Sun → Summer. When it tilts away → Winter.
